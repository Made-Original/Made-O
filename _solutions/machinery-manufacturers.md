## The challenge

Machinery and industrial equipment is complex: assemblies, sub-assemblies, options, spare parts, drawings and manuals, often configured differently for each customer. When this information sits in CAD files, ERP records and folders of PDFs, engineers spend hours searching, service teams guess at part numbers, and customers wait.

## How we help

- **Component and spare-parts data.** Structured libraries of components and spare parts, linked to the machines they belong to.
- **Machine documentation.** Manuals, drawings, certificates and maintenance procedures organised per machine and per configuration.
- **Machinery knowledge platform.** One searchable place for your teams, dealers and customers to find documentation, parts and technical data.
- **Customer delivery portal.** Customers see the documents and parts for exactly the machines they bought.
- **Export and compliance.** Documentation prepared for each market, including the [EU Machinery Regulation](/services/technical-documentation/).

## Who we work with

Mechanical engineering companies, industrial equipment makers and builders of engineered-to-order machinery, especially those selling through dealers or exporting to Europe and other international markets.
