Made Original helps export-oriented pump, machinery and engineering manufacturers get their product data, documentation and dealer systems ready for international markets. Our consulting ends in working systems, not slide decks.

We are based in Kakkanad, Kochi, and work with manufacturers across India and worldwide.

We start with your products and the people who use the data: dealers, engineers and customers. We build a working prototype early, test it with real users, and scale what works. Our solutions are built on [NovaDB](/#technology), an enterprise product-information platform by Noxum.
