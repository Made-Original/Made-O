## Why product data comes first

Every export conversation depends on product data: the specifications in a quote, the data sheet a distributor asks for, the certificate a customs officer checks. If that data lives in spreadsheets, ERP fields and engineers' heads, each answer is slow and each catalogue is slightly different. Clean, structured product data is what everything else is built on.

## What we do

- **Audit.** We map where your product information lives today, who uses it and where it breaks.
- **Data model.** We design a structure for your range: product families, variants, attributes, units, documents and relationships.
- **Clean and enrich.** We consolidate duplicates, fill gaps, standardise units and naming, and attach the right documents and images to each product.
- **Translate and localise.** We prepare the data for each market and language you sell into.
- **Connect.** We set up the product information system and connect it to your catalogue, website, dealer portal and documentation.

## What you get

- one trusted source for every product, variant and document
- consistent data sheets and catalogues in every language
- faster answers to technical enquiries from dealers and customers
- a foundation for [dealer portals](/services/dealer-portals/), [technical documentation](/services/technical-documentation/) and selection tools like [Pump Connect](/pump-connect/)

## Who it is for

Medium and large pump, machinery and engineering manufacturers with complex ranges (many variants, technical attributes and documents) who are selling, or planning to sell, in international markets.
