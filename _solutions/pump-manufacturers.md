## Where pump manufacturers lose time

Pump sales are technical. Every enquiry starts with a duty point, a liquid, a material and a standard, and ends with a curve, a data sheet and a quote. In many pump companies, that information is spread across curve files, price lists, spreadsheets and the memory of a few senior engineers. Dealers wait, quotes go out late, and the wrong pump is sometimes specified.

## How we help

- **Structured pump data.** Models, sizes, impellers, motors, materials of construction and options in one consistent database.
- **Performance curves and selection.** Curves stored as data, not images, so dealers and customers can select pumps by duty point.
- **Digital catalogues.** Current catalogues and price lists for every market, instead of printed PDFs.
- **Dealer portal.** Dealers find products, pricing, curves, manuals and certificates in one login.
- **Export documentation.** Manuals, declarations and certificates organised and translated for each market.
