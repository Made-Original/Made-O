## What Pump Connect does

Pump buyers and dealers ask the same questions every day: which pump meets this duty point, what is the material of construction, and where is the curve and the manual. When that information is spread across PDFs, spreadsheets and engineers' inboxes, every answer takes days. Pump Connect puts it in one place.

- **Structured pump data.** Every model, size, impeller, motor rating, material grade and document is stored once, in a consistent format, ready for every market and language you sell into.
- **Guided selection.** Dealers and customers enter flow, head and application, and get the pumps that fit, with performance curves and duty point shown.
- **A digital catalogue.** Always current, searchable, and available on any device, replacing printed price lists and emailed PDFs.
- **A dealer portal.** Dealers log in to find products, pricing, data sheets, manuals and certificates for exactly what they sell.

## Who it is for

Pump Connect is built for pump manufacturers who sell through dealers and distributors, in India and abroad, and who want to:

- answer technical enquiries faster than competitors
- stop dealers quoting from outdated price lists
- get export-ready product data and documentation for new markets
- give engineers back the time they spend searching for curves and drawings

## How it works

We start by mapping your pump range and where the data lives today. We then build a working version with one real product series, so your team and a few dealers can click through it and tell us what is missing. From there we roll out the rest of the range and connect it to the systems you already use.

Pump Connect runs on [NovaDB](/#technology), an enterprise product-information platform, so it scales from a single catalogue to your full range and dealer network.
